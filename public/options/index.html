Translation service

Enter a service key and load a model. Your settings are saved automatically when all three fields are complete.

Your key is stored only on this device and is used to connect to the selected translation service.

Caption display

Choose how captions appear in videos. Changes apply to the current video immediately.

When disabled, the translation button is hidden in Shorts. Comment translation remains available.

Drag captions vertically in the player to avoid important content. Their position and style are saved automatically.

Selection translation

When disabled, selecting text will not show the translation panel and the context menu will not start a translation.

When enabled, the surrounding sentences help produce a more natural translation.
